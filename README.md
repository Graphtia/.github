What we're building

Modern codebases are becoming harder to understand as they grow across thousands of files, dependencies, services, tests, and years of accumulated decisions.

At the same time, AI coding agents are becoming capable of taking on larger engineering tasks — but they still need better structural context, continuity, and governance to work reliably.

Graphtia is building tools for both.

Graphtia Governance

Persistent governance for agentic software engineering.

Graphtia Governance is being designed for longer-running AI engineering workflows where maintaining context, plans, boundaries, and execution state matters.

Its focus is helping agentic development stay structured across complex work rather than treating every interaction as an isolated coding task.

Areas of focus include:

Persistent engineering context

Structured planning and execution

Governed agent workflows

Task and workspace continuity

Verification and evidence

Multi-agent coordination

Reliable long-running development workflows

Why Graphtia

Software engineering is changing.

Developers increasingly work alongside AI agents, but better code generation alone does not solve the hardest problems in large software systems.

Agents need to understand:

what exists

how it connects

what a change affects

what happened before

what constraints still apply

what work remains

Graphtia is building an engineering layer around that context.
